## Findings and decisions (2026-10-11)

**Both PRD decision targets are met on the first run:** routing accuracy 96% (≥ 90%) and action
requests answered with an article **0%** (≤ 5%). The rule-based action signal (`signals.py`) put
every one of the 36 action requests on the SR path, and LLM field extraction labelled every action
row correctly (25 RAISE_SR, 11 COLLECT_DETAILS, no extraction failures). Rule 6 fired exactly where
intended: the production restart (act-11) and the three commission disputes.

Thresholds are unchanged from the Day 3 re-calibration (high 0.56, mid 0.49, router_min 0.46).

**The three wrong decisions, and why they are left for now:**

- **oos-03 "when is salary credited"** and **oos-14 "when is the next payroll run"** get an answer
  with a disclaimer instead of an escalation. Both are questions whose wording sits close to the
  commission article (dense 0.545 / 0.504, above mid). The disclaimer offers a person, and the
  generator is told to say when the context doesn't answer the question — a soft failure, not a
  wrong SR. Raising `confidence_mid` above 0.545 would fix them but push the error-code questions
  (PWD-1407 0.497, CRM-5503 0.512) into escalation; not worth it on two rows.
- **inf-24 "what does DNS-3310 mean"** escalates. BM25 retrieves the right article at #1, but the
  dense score (0.382) is below mid and the router isn't confident. The confidence signal is
  dense-only, so it under-rates exact-code lookups. **Day 5 candidate:** let a strong BM25 hit on
  an exact code count as confidence — measured on the eval set before adopting.

**Latency (new finding).** The decision rules cost ~0 ms. The cost is in the model calls:
embedding p50 2.5 s, and on the SR path the field extraction adds p50 2.0 s / p95 3.6 s, so raising
an SR takes ~5 s end to end. The PRD's 3 s target is for the answer path, but this feeds the Day 5
latency decision (plan-days-4-5.md step 5).
