# Policy endorsements and corrections

Any change to an issued policy is an endorsement. Endorsements are versioned: the policy keeps its
number and gains an endorsement sequence, so the full history stays auditable.

## What can be endorsed

Non-financial endorsements do not change the premium: name spelling correction, date of birth
correction, address change, contact details, nominee change, and bank account for premium debit.
These are processed within two business days.

Financial endorsements change the premium or the risk: sum assured increase or decrease, policy term
change, addition or removal of a rider, and for motor, a change of vehicle or coverage type. These
need underwriter review and take five to seven business days. An increase in sum assured may
require fresh medicals, which restarts the clock.

## What cannot be endorsed

The life assured on a life policy can never be changed — that is a different person and therefore a
different policy. The policy commencement date cannot be changed once premium has been realised. A
lapsed policy cannot be endorsed until it is revived; revival is a separate process with its own
underwriting.

A date of birth change that moves the insured into a different age band is treated as a financial
endorsement, because it changes the premium, and may be declined if it would have made the original
policy ineligible.

## Required details

An endorsement request needs the policy number, the field being changed, and the new value. All
three are mandatory. "Update the customer's details" cannot be actioned — operations will not infer
which field, and guessing on a policy document is not acceptable.

Supporting proof is required for the identity fields: a date of birth change needs a birth
certificate, passport or PAN; a name correction needs PAN or Aadhaar showing the correct spelling;
a nominee change needs a signed nomination form; an address change needs a recent utility bill or
Aadhaar.

## Approval

All endorsements need approval. Non-financial ones are approved by the operations team lead;
financial ones additionally need an underwriter. Nominee changes on policies with an assignment
recorded need the assignee's consent, which is usually a bank where the policy is collateral.

## After processing

An endorsement letter is issued and sent to the customer's registered address and email, and the
policy document in the portal is reissued at the new version. The endorsement takes effect from the
date it is processed, not the date it was requested, which matters for a mid-term sum assured
increase.

## Common errors

PAS-4401 means the policy is lapsed and must be revived first. PAS-4405 means the field is locked
for endorsement on this product. PAS-4412 means a prior endorsement is still in progress — only one
endorsement can be open on a policy at a time, and the second must wait for the first to complete.
