# Commission payout schedule and disputes

Commission for a given month is credited on the 7th of the following month. If the 7th falls on a
Sunday or a public holiday, the credit moves to the next working day.

## The cycle

The commission period runs from the 1st to the last day of each calendar month. The cut-off for a
policy to count toward a month is the policy issuance date, not the proposal date and not the
payment date. A policy issued on the 31st counts for that month; one issued on the 1st of the next
month does not.

Statements are published in the agent portal on the 3rd of the following month, four days before the
credit. The statement is the authoritative record — the portal's running commission figure during
the month is indicative and will move as policies issue, lapse or are returned.

## What gets held back

Commission is only paid on policies that have cleared their free-look period. A policy issued on the
25th with a 15-day free-look does not clear until the 10th of the next month, so its commission
appears in the following cycle rather than the immediate one. This is the most common reason an
expected amount is missing, and it is not an error.

Commission on a policy returned within the free-look window is reversed. If it had already been
paid, it is recovered against the next payout.

## Rates

| Product | First year | Renewal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Term life | 25% of premium | 5% years 2-5, 2% thereafter |
| Health indemnity | 15% of premium | 7.5% |
| Motor, private car | 10% of premium | 10% |
| Motor, commercial | 7.5% of premium | 7.5% |

Rates are set by the regulator's caps and are not negotiable per agent. A regional override can
apply during a declared campaign; campaign terms are published separately and are time-bound.

## Deductions

TDS is deducted at 5% where PAN is on record, and at 20% where it is not. An agent with a missing
PAN sees a materially smaller credit, and the fix is to update PAN with operations — the earlier
deduction is not refundable by us, it is claimed back when filing returns.

Any outstanding advance against commission is recovered before the credit, as is any free-look
reversal from a prior cycle.

## Checking a payout

The statement in the agent portal shows, per policy: policy number, issuance date, premium,
applicable rate, gross commission, free-look status, and whether it is included in this cycle or
held. Most questions are answered by the free-look column.

## Raising a dispute

A dispute needs the agent code and the month in question. Disputes are accepted where the statement
shows a wrong rate, a policy missing entirely from a cycle it should have been in, or a deduction
that cannot be accounted for. Disputes require approval because they can result in an off-cycle
payment.

A query that is simply "where is my money" for a policy still inside its free-look window is not a
dispute and is answered with the schedule above. Disputes must be raised within 90 days of the
statement date; after that the cycle is closed for adjustment.
