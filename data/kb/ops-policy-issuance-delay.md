# Policy issuance timelines and delays

A proposal becomes an issued policy once underwriting clears it and the first premium is realised.
Most proposals issue within three business days; anything past that is treated as a delay worth
chasing.

## Standard timelines

| Product | Normal issuance time |
| --- | --- |
| Term life, no medicals required | 1-2 business days |
| Term life, medicals required | 5-7 business days from the medical appointment |
| Health indemnity, under 45, no pre-existing conditions | 2-3 business days |
| Health indemnity, over 45 or declared conditions | 7-10 business days |
| Motor, private car | Same day |
| Motor, commercial vehicle | 2-3 business days |

The clock starts when the proposal is complete, not when it was first submitted. A proposal missing
a document has not started its timeline, which is why a proposal can appear to be sitting for a week
while the system considers it not yet begun.

## Why issuance stalls

The common causes, in rough order of frequency: a document is missing or illegible; the premium
payment has not realised, which for a cheque means up to three business days of clearing; the
medical appointment has not been attended; a declared pre-existing condition requires underwriter
review; or the nominee details are incomplete.

Payment realisation is the single biggest cause. A payment showing as "received" in the agent portal
is not the same as realised — received means the instruction arrived, realised means the funds
cleared. Issuance waits for realised.

## Checking status

The proposal status is visible in the agent portal against the proposal number. The statuses are:
submitted, under review, pending documents, pending medicals, pending payment, approved, and issued.
A proposal in "pending documents" will not move until the document arrives, no matter how long it
sits, and chasing it without supplying the document achieves nothing.

## Free-look period

Every issued policy carries a 15-day free-look period from the date the policy document is received
by the customer, 30 days if the policy was sold through distance marketing. During the free-look
window the customer can return the policy for a refund of premium less medical and stamp duty
costs. This matters for commission, which is only credited after the free-look window closes.

## When to raise a request

Raise an issuance delay request when the proposal is past three business days in "under review" or
"approved" without issuing, or when the portal status contradicts what the customer has been told.
Supply the policy or proposal number — without it the request cannot be investigated.

Do not raise one for a proposal in "pending documents", "pending medicals" or "pending payment".
Those are waiting on the customer, not on operations, and the request will be closed with that
explanation.
