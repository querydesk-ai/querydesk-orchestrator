## Status notifications (hand-measured)

10 approval SRs were created on the stand-in (`it_uat_extension`) and moved to APPROVED. Each
time was measured from the `PATCH` to the notification appearing in `GET /notifications`. The path
is: signed webhook (integer `sr_id`, contract v1.1) → `POST /webhooks/sr-status` → Celery worker
(`--pool=solo`) → Neon → API.

| Within 60 s | Min | Median | Max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 / 10 | 2.8 s | 3.1 s | 8.3 s |

The first SR took 8.3 s on the worker's cold start; the other nine took 2.8–3.3 s. Lost webhooks
are covered by `reconcile` every 10 minutes (verified on Day 7), which is outside the 1-minute
target by design.

## Latency: the answer path end to end

Measured from the `querydesk.decision` log lines of the 20 answers above. This is a laptop on a
home connection, with Fireworks, Qdrant Cloud, Upstash and Neon all remote; 1 of 20 was a query
cache hit.

| Stage | p50 | p95 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Embed (Fireworks qwen3, plus BM25) | 2273 ms | 2561 ms |
| Search + route (concurrent) | 1161 ms | 1583 ms |
| Generate (Fireworks minimax-m3) | 2312 ms | 4315 ms |
| **Total** | **5758 ms** | **7862 ms** |

**The p95 < 3 s target is missed by a wide margin.**

- **Embedding alone takes most of the budget.** The local alternative was trialled and rejected,
  because it routes worse ([embedding-trial.md](embedding-trial.md)).
- **Generation is about twice the Day 2 figure** (~1.2 s then).
- **In-app search is slower than in the eval loop** (268 ms p50 there). The eval measures search
  alone; in the app it runs concurrently with the router.

Mitigations in place:

- **Query cache:** a repeated message embeds in 30–60 ms.
- **BM25 model loaded at startup.**
- **No LLM call** when there is nothing to ground on.

What could still close the gap (not done, each needs its own measurement):

- deploying next to the Qdrant region (Day 10);
- streaming the answer, so the first words arrive sooner (the full answer wouldn't);
- a faster chat model, re-checked against answer support.
